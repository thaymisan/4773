Dinner
Sheet Pan Lemon Herb Salmon + Rice
25 min
Ingredients
2 salmon fillets · 1 lemon sliced · 2 garlic cloves minced · olive oil · dried oregano · salt & pepper · microwave rice pouches
Steps
  1. Preheat oven 400°F.
  2. Place salmon on lined pan, drizzle olive oil, top with garlic, lemon, oregano, salt & pepper.
  3. Bake 12–15 min until flaky. Microwave rice. Serve together.
💡 Microwave rice pouches save time on a busy day with an early appointment.
